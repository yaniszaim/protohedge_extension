Review-anonymity notes
----------------------
The reviewer-facing source and PDF display "Anonymous Authors" and contain no
author names, affiliations, email addresses, acknowledgments that identify the
authors, or links to an author repository. Do not add author details until the
camera-ready stage, and do not upload the broader research archive because its
provenance records contain identifying local paths.

Compliance notes
----------------
The final paragraph of the Introduction states that code and redistributable
artifacts will be released upon acceptance and explains the licensing limit on
the underlying OptionMetrics and CRSP records. The Acknowledgment discloses
Codex-assisted software development, drafting, and language editing and cites
the system, as required by the venue's AI-content policy. After acceptance,
replace the release pledge with the permanent artifact link.

Formatting notes
----------------
The source uses \documentclass[10pt,conference]{IEEEtran}. IEEEtran.cls is
provided by Overleaf and standard IEEE submission systems, so it is not bundled.
The compiled manuscript is six pages total, with the conclusion,
Acknowledgment, and references sharing page 6. This is within the allowance of
up to six content pages plus one references page. \IEEEoverridecommandlockouts
is intentionally omitted because the anonymous manuscript has no first-page
funding footnote.

The submission intentionally includes one results figure. The earlier QQQ
top-prototype illustration was removed because a single decision snapshot is
anecdotal and does not validate human interpretability. The manuscript instead
states the narrower property established by the architecture: every proposed
PH trade has an exact, model-native decomposition into prototype weights and
attached actions. Prototype-use statistics remain available in the full
research archive as diagnostics, not as primary evidence.

---------------------------
- Select the full-paper track and the intended symposium.
- Make the title and abstract fields match main.pdf exactly.
- Enter every author and verify the final author order; no author changes are
  permitted after acceptance.
- Each author must update and confirm the "My Conflicts" page, even when there
  are no conflicts. Include current and previous institutions from the last
  three years, recent collaborators and joint-grant partners, advisor/advisee
  relationships, close personal relationships, and financial/business ties.
- Confirm that every listed author made a significant contribution and approved
  the submitted version.
- Confirm that the work is original and is not published, submitted, or under
  review elsewhere.
- Upload only the anonymous main.pdf for peer review. Do not upload an
  identified source, public author repository, or provenance-rich research
  archive as supplementary material.
- If accepted, submit the camera-ready paper by the stated deadline, register,
  and arrange for at least one author to present it.
